Tappable card-row with a radio or checkbox indicator; replaces native checkboxes and radios everywhere in the app.

```jsx
<OptionRow icon="dumbbell" title="Prensa de piernas" subtitle="Disponible en tu gimnasio" multi selected={on} onClick={toggle} />
```

`multi` switches the indicator to a square checkbox. Selected rows get the amber-soft fill and amber hairline.
